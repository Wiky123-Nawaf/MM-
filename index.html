Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MM+ ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related higher-education advisory services.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate university program comparisons, student eligibility evaluation, official admissions, and counselling consultations, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Account and Profile Information

  • Basic Identity: Full legal name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and nationality.
  • Academic History: Current education level, prior school/institution name, graduation year, GPA/grade transcripts, and English language test qualifications (e.g., IELTS, TOEFL, MUET).
  • Preferences: Preferred study destination countries, fields of study, degree levels, and annual tuition budgets.

B. Verification & KYC Documents

  • Identity Documents: National ID cards and passport scans uploaded by you to verify your identity for university application processing.
  • Academic Documents: Official transcripts, graduation certificates, and language proficiency test certificates.
  • Biometric Selfie Capture: We utilize on-device face detection during registration to confirm live user identity and generate an official passport photograph for your university admission record. We do not sell or monetize biometric imagery.

C. Audio and Voice Data

  • Voice Messages & Audio Input: When you record voice notes to communicate with university counsellors or use our AI Voice Advisor, audio files are processed to deliver the message or transcribe queries via voice-to-text. Audio is only accessed with explicit microphone permission.

D. Location Information

  • With your permission, we access approximate or precise device location to display universities nearest to you on interactive campus maps and to suggest relevant local and international campuses.

E. Payment Information

  • When paying university enrolment fees or tuition deposits, payments are securely processed through regulated payment gateways (e.g., Billplz / FPX / Online Banking). We do not store sensitive credit card or banking credentials on our servers.

2. Device Permissions Used by MM+

MM+ requests the following Android permissions strictly for app functionality:

  • CAMERA: To perform live face verification and take applicant passport photos.
  • RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: To record voice notes for counsellors and enable voice search in the AI advisor.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To synchronize your university applications, chat with counsellors, and communicate with secure cloud services.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & VIBRATE: To deliver real-time notifications about application status updates, interview invitations, voucher rewards, and upcoming deadlines.
  • BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: To route audio properly through connected headsets during counsellor video consultations.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide educational services, including:

  • Creating and managing your student account.
  • Evaluating academic eligibility and presenting personalized university and scholarship recommendations.
  • Transmitting verified application packets and supporting documents to your chosen educational institutions.
  • Facilitating secure live messaging and video consultations between you, assigned counsellors, and invited guardians.
  • Processing tuition, registration, and enrolment receipts.
  • Providing intelligent automated admissions guidance via our AI Advisor.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party cloud and infrastructure providers who adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure authentication, real-time database storage (Firestore), encrypted document storage, and push notifications (FCM).
  • Google Maps & OpenStreetMap: Used to display university campus coordinates and location discovery.
  • OpenAI: Used to interpret natural language student queries and provide program recommendations based strictly on registered MM+ database data.
  • Billplz Payment Gateway: Used for secure local and international tuition fee and deposit settlements.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS in transit and AES-256 at rest) to safeguard your personal data and documents. We retain your profile and application documentation for as long as your account remains active or as required by university admission regulatory frameworks.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your personal data. You have the right to:

  • Access, review, and update your personal information directly within the app settings.
  • Replace or delete uploaded documents.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team at dokturamedic@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for students exploring secondary and tertiary higher-education opportunities (typically aged 16 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental or guardian participation.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
